Bets and payouts

Bet an Ante and two cards are dealt face up. The spread is the number of ranks between them. Raise to double your bet, or call, and the third card is dealt: if it falls between the first two, the Ante and the Raise are both paid by the spread.

Three of a kind11 TO 1

Two cards in a row are a push without a third card. A pair always draws the third card, and you cannot raise it: three of a kind pays 11 to 1, anything else is a push.

House edge with the best strategy: 2.75% of the Ante. Calling every hand: 7.88%.

Eight decks, shuffled for every hand. The ace is always high, and a card of either rank is out. Amounts use whole dollars.
