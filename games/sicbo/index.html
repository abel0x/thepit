    Bets and payouts

    Three dice decide every bet on the layout at once. Small wins on totals of 4 to 10 and Big on 11 to 17; Odd and Even win on the total. All four lose to any triple.

    A total pays when the three dice add up to it. A triple pays when all three dice show its number, Any triple on any of the six. A double pays when at least two dice show its number, a combination when its two numbers both appear. A single number pays 1 to 1 for each die that shows it.

    Every bet is settled by the roll it was made for: winners are paid with their stake back and the layout clears for the next roll. Rebet puts the last bets down again.
