Bets and payouts

Bet an Ante, and an AA Bonus if you like. You and the dealer get two cards each and the flop is turned. Call with twice the Ante to see the turn and the river, or fold and give up the Ante.

The best five of your two cards and the board play against the dealer's. The dealer plays with a pair of fours or better. If not, the Ante pays by your hand and the Call is returned. If so, the better hand wins the Ante by your hand and the Call at 1 to 1, and a tie returns both.
