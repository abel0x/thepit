    Boards and payouts

    Choose the rows of pegs, the risk and a bet, then drop. At every row the ball bounces left or right, each with an even chance, and lands in a slot at the bottom that pays its multiple of the bet.

    Drop 1, 5, 10 or 20 balls at a time, each with the bet. Every ball's way down is drawn and saved before the first one falls, and they are paid together when the last one lands.

    Return of every board
    RowsLowMediumHigh

    Every return is counted exactly over all the ways down a board: 256 for 8 rows, 4,096 for 12 and 65,536 for 16. A higher risk spreads about the same return over fewer, larger pays.

    Bets are whole tens of dollars, so every slot pays whole dollars. The rows and the risk change between drops.
