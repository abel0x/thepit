How to play and pays

Mark 1 to 10 of the 80 numbers, choose a bet with a chip and play: the machine draws 20 numbers at random. You are paid by how many of your marks are caught, from the pay table for the number of marks.

Pays are for 1 and include the bet: 15 for 1 returns 15 times the bet. Your marks stay on the ticket, so Play again draws for the same numbers. Quick pick marks as many numbers as you have marked, or 10 on an empty ticket.

Each return is exact, from the odds of the draw. With whole-dollar pays a ticket of 1 mark returns less than the others.
