    Bets and payouts

    Pass wins on 7 or 11 when coming out, loses on 2, 3 or 12; any other total is the point, which wins if it comes again before a 7. Don't pass is the reverse, with 12 a tie. Come and Don't come work the same way from the roll after they are made.

    Odds pay the true odds behind a pass or come bet once there is a point: up to 3 times the bet on 4 and 10, 4 times on 5 and 9, 5 times on 6 and 8. Laid odds behind don't bets go up to 6 times the bet.

    Place bets and Hardways do not work on the come-out roll, nor do the odds behind a come bet. Field and the bets in the middle are decided by the next roll.

    Amounts use whole dollars and payouts round down: bet Place 6 and 8 in sixes and Place 5 and 9 in fives to be paid in full. A winning Place, Field, Hardway or middle bet stays up for the next roll.

    House edges

    Pass and Come 1.41% · Don't pass and Don't come 1.36% · Odds 0% · Place 6 and 8 1.52% · Place 5 and 9 4.00% · Place 4 and 10 6.67% · Field 2.78% · Hard 6 and 8 9.09% · Hard 4 and 10 11.11% · Any seven 16.67% · Any craps, 3 and 11 11.11% · 2 and 12 13.89%

    Put: once a point is set, a come bet can be put straight on any number with PUT. It wins even money when the number comes before a 7 and takes odds like a come bet, but it skips the come-out, so its own edge is 9.09% on 6 and 8, 20.00% on 5 and 9 and 33.33% on 4 and 10.

    Hop bets win on one roll of the exact pair of dice: 30 to 1 for a pair, a 13.89% edge, and 15 to 1 for two different faces, 11.11%. The 2, 3, 11 and 12 have spots of their own.

    The Fire Bet, up to $25, goes down before a new shooter makes a point and counts the different points made before the seven out: 4 points pay 24 to 1 and 5 pay 249 to 1 at the seven out, all 6 pay 999 to 1 the moment the last is made. Fewer than 4 lose. Its edge is 20.76%.
