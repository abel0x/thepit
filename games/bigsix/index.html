    Bets and payouts

    Bet on a symbol, then spin: when the wheel stops, the bet on the symbol under the pointer wins and every other bet loses. A dollar symbol pays its number to 1; the Joker and the logo pay 40 to 1.

    The wheel has 54 segments: 24 of $1, 15 of $2, 7 of $5, 4 of $10, 2 of $20, one Joker and one logo. Each segment is as likely as any other.

    Every bet is settled by the spin it was made for: winners are paid with their stake back and the layout clears for the next spin. Rebet puts the last bets down again.
