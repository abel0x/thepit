Table rules

Rules change between coups. A tie returns Player and Banker bets. A Banker win is paid at even money and its 5% commission goes in the commission box, to the cent; each whole dollar there is taken from a later payout, so every stake pays exactly 19 to 20. Without commission, the half dollar of an odd stake on a Banker six waits in the box for you.

The Dragon Bonus

A natural that wins pays 1 to 1, and a tie of naturals is returned. A hand that wins without a natural pays by its margin: 9 points 30 to 1, 8 points 10 to 1, 7 points 6 to 1, 6 points 4 to 1, 5 points 2 to 1, 4 points 1 to 1. A smaller win loses.

Counted over the whole eight-deck shoe, the house keeps 2.65% of the Player dragon and 9.37% of the Banker dragon.

Who draws a third card

With 8 or 9 on either side nobody draws (a natural). Otherwise the Player draws on 0 to 5 and stands on 6 or 7.

If the Player stood, the Banker draws on 0 to 5. If the Player drew, the Banker's third card depends on its total and the Player's third card, as at every Punto Banco table.
