How to play

Pick a coin value and bet one to five coins, then deal five cards. Hold any of them and draw once: every card you let go is replaced from the same deck. The hand you end with pays by the table.

Pays are in coins for the coins bet, the bet included. In Jacks or Better and Double Bonus a pair pays only when it is Jacks or better, and Double Bonus gives only the bet back for two pair.

In Deuces Wild every two stands for whatever card makes the best hand. A natural royal flush has no twos in it, a wild royal does. A pair or two pair pays nothing.

A natural royal flush pays 250 a coin, and 800 a coin when all five coins are bet.

With the best play on every hand and five coins bet, Jacks or Better returns 99.54% at 9/6 and 97.30% at 8/5, Double Bonus 99.11% and Deuces Wild 99.73%. With fewer coins the return is lower.

One deck, shuffled for every hand. Amounts use whole dollars.
