Bets and payouts

Bet an Ante, and a Pair Plus if you like, then see your three cards. Play by matching the Ante with a Play bet, or fold and give up the Ante and the Pair Plus.

The dealer plays with Queen high or better. If not, the Ante pays 1 to 1 and the Play bet is returned. If so, the better hand wins both at 1 to 1, and a tie returns both.

The 6-Card Bonus pays the best five-card poker hand among your three cards and the dealer's three, whether you play or fold. Its limit is $100, and over all 20,358,520 six-card deals the house keeps 8.56%.

House edge with the best strategy: 3.37% of the Ante on Ante and Play, 2.32% on Pair Plus.

One deck, shuffled for every hand. In three-card poker a straight beats a flush, and A-2-3 is the lowest straight. Amounts use whole dollars.
