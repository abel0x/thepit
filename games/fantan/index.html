    Bets and payouts

    Bet on the numbers 1 to 4. The croupier takes a pile of beads and counts them off four at a time; the one to four beads left at the end are the number that wins.

    Fan wins on its number. Kwok wins on the two numbers at a corner, Odd on 1 and 3, Even on 2 and 4, and Sheh Sam Hong on three numbers. Nim wins on its first number and is returned on its second; Nga Tan wins on its first two and is returned on its third.

    Every win is paid its odds less 5% commission. The whole dollars are paid at once and the change waits in the box for a later win, so every bet is paid exactly its odds less 5%.

    House edge: Fan 3.75%; Nim, Kwok, Odd and Even 2.50%; Nga Tan and Sheh Sam Hong 1.25%.

    The pile holds 41 to 104 beads, drawn on your device: each of the four numbers is left by sixteen of the sixty-four piles.
